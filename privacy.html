Privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

What the app accesses

obsidian-sync requests only the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It can see and change only the files and folders it created itself, in a folder named "Obsidian-Sync". It cannot read any other files on your Google Drive.

How data is used

The app uploads your Obsidian notes to your own Google Drive and downloads them to your other devices. That is its only purpose.

Where data is stored

Notes are stored on your devices and in your own Google Drive. The sign-in token is stored only in the plugin settings on your devices. The developer has no server and does not receive, store or see your data.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the "Obsidian-Sync" folder from your Google Drive.
